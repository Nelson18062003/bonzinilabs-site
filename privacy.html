Privacy policy

BONZINILABS LTD · Last updated 6 August 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data

BONZINILABS LTD is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. We are a company registered in England & Wales, registered office 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, WC2H 9JQ, United Kingdom. For any question about your data, write to contact@bonzinilabs.com.

2. What this website collects

This website is deliberately minimal. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no third-party scripts, and it sets no cookies.

  • Language preference. If you switch the page between English and French, that single choice is stored in your browser’s local storage so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device and we cannot read it. Clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Contact form. The form does not send anything to us or to any server. It opens your own email application with the message pre-filled; nothing is transmitted until you choose to send that email.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records technical data such as IP address, time of request and browser type in order to serve the page and protect the service against abuse. We use this only for security and reliability.

3. Data we hold when you contact us or become a client

  • Enquiries: your name, email address, company and whatever you write to us.
  • Clients: contact details of the people we work with, proposals and contracts, project correspondence, and billing and payment records.

4. Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To answer your enquiry and prepare a proposal — our legitimate interest in responding to a business request, and steps taken at your request before a contract.
  • To deliver, support and invoice the services — performance of our contract with you.
  • To keep accounting and tax records — our legal obligations in the United Kingdom.
  • To keep our systems secure — our legitimate interest in protecting the service.

We do not sell personal data, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.

5. Who we share it with

Only with service providers that help us operate, each bound to protect it and to use it solely on our instructions: our website host, our email provider, our accounting software and our payment provider. Card details are handled by the payment provider directly — we never receive them. We also disclose data where the law requires it.

6. Where your data is held

Our providers may process data outside the United Kingdom. Where that happens we rely on the appropriate safeguards permitted by UK data protection law, such as UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

7. Client data inside the systems we build

When we build an AI agent, chatbot or automation for you, that system may process personal data belonging to your customers or staff. For that data you are the controller and we act as your processor: we process it only on your documented instructions, for the purposes of the engagement, and we can enter into a data processing agreement setting this out. We do not use your data or your customers’ data for any other client, nor to train models for anyone else.

8. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to work: up to 12 months.
  • Client records and correspondence: for the engagement and 6 years afterwards.
  • Invoices and accounting records: 6 years after the end of the relevant financial year, as UK law requires.
  • Server logs: for the short retention period set by our hosting provider.

9. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you may ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or provide it in a portable format. Where we rely on consent you may withdraw it at any time. Write to contact@bonzinilabs.com and we will respond within one month.

If you are not satisfied with our answer you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office, the UK supervisory authority, at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

10. Security

We apply access controls, encryption in transit, multi-factor authentication on the accounts we use, and the principle of least privilege. No system is perfectly secure, but we take the protection of your information seriously and will tell you promptly if a breach affects you.

11. Children

Our services are sold to businesses and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data about anyone under 16 through this website.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change, and the date at the top always shows the current version.
