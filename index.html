Services

What we build for our clients

Four service lines, sold as projects or as a monthly plan. Each one starts from a process you already run and ends with software your team uses every day.

Custom AI agents

We build an assistant that reads your documents, answers staff questions and updates your tools, so routine requests are resolved without someone stopping to look things up.

  • Trained on your own documents
  • Takes actions in your tools
  • Hands over to a person

Intelligent chatbots

A support and sales assistant on your website and messaging channels that answers common questions day and night, and passes real opportunities to your team.

  • Answers drawn from your content
  • Works in several languages
  • Connects to your CRM or helpdesk

WhatsApp Business automation

We set up the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API): order updates, appointment reminders and payment follow-ups sent automatically, on the channel your customers already open every day.

  • Cloud API setup and templates
  • Automated flows with AI replies
  • Shared inbox for your team

Business process automation

We connect the systems your team retypes data between — orders, quotes, invoices, spreadsheets, CRM — so the handover happens on its own and nothing waits in an inbox.

  • Maps your current workflow first
  • Builds the integrations between tools
  • Monitoring and fixes after launch
How we work

From first call to running system

  1. 01

    Scoped proposal

    We map the process, the tools and the volumes involved, then send a written proposal with a fixed scope, a fixed price and a delivery date.

  2. 02

    Build cycles

    We develop in short cycles and show you a working version at each milestone, so you test the real system on your own data before anything goes live.

  3. 03

    Launch support

    We deploy the system, train the people who use it and stay available afterwards; monitoring, changes and improvements run on a monthly support plan.

Engagement

How we scope and bill

Every project is quoted individually. After a scoping call we issue a written proposal with a fixed scope and a fixed price, or a monthly plan for ongoing support and improvements; work starts once the proposal is accepted. Invoices are issued by BONZINILABS LTD in GBP or EUR and settled by bank transfer or card.

We sell professional services to businesses only. We do not sell physical goods, downloadable products or self-serve consumer subscriptions.

Card payments are processed by our payment provider. BONZINILABS LTD never sees or stores your full card details.

We do not provide services to individuals or entities subject to UK, EU, US or UN sanctions, and we do not supply our software to sanctioned jurisdictions.

About

A UK technology company focused on applied AI

BONZINILABS LTD is a technology company registered in England and Wales, with its registered office in London. We specialise in applied artificial intelligence and automation for businesses: custom AI agents, customer-facing chatbots, WhatsApp Business Platform integrations and the connections between the systems a company already runs every day.

We work with owners and operations leads at small and mid-sized companies — agencies, e-commerce brands, service providers and local operators — across Europe and Africa. They are rarely technical, so we describe what a system will do in plain terms, agree it in writing, and keep the software understandable to the people who run it.

Our commercial model is straightforward. We sell professional services to businesses, not products to consumers: each engagement is either a fixed-scope project invoiced against a written proposal, or a monthly support and improvement plan. Work is delivered remotely, in English and French, and every client receives an invoice issued by BONZINILABS LTD.
