Refunds, cancellation & delivery

BONZINILABS LTD · Last updated 6 August 2026

1. What this policy covers

BONZINILABS LTD (“we”, “us”) provides software development and automation services to business clients. This policy explains how we deliver our work, how an engagement can be cancelled, and when a fee is refunded. It applies to every engagement unless the accepted proposal or statement of work says otherwise, in which case that document takes precedence.

We do not sell physical goods, downloadable products or self-serve consumer subscriptions, so no returns policy applies.

2. How work is agreed

No work begins, and no fee becomes payable, before you have accepted a written proposal or statement of work from BONZINILABS LTD. That document states the scope, the deliverables, the price, the currency and the delivery dates. Anything outside that scope is quoted separately and only starts once you have accepted it in writing.

3. Delivery

All services are delivered remotely, by electronic means — there is no physical shipment.

  • Project work is delivered against the milestones set out in the accepted proposal.
  • Each milestone is made available to you for review, in a working environment you can test.
  • Monthly support plans run from the start date stated in the plan and are delivered continuously for the duration of the plan.
  • Where a delivery date depends on information, access or approvals from you, the date moves by the time we wait for them. We will tell you when that happens.

4. Acceptance and corrections

You have 10 business days from the delivery of a milestone to tell us, in writing, that a deliverable does not match the agreed specification. If it does not, we will correct it at no additional charge. If we cannot bring it into line with the agreed specification, you may choose a refund of the fee for that deliverable instead.

5. Cancelling a project

  • You may cancel a project at any time by writing to contact@bonzinilabs.com.
  • You are invoiced for the milestones already delivered and accepted, plus any work in progress on the current milestone.
  • Any amount you have paid in advance for milestones that have not been started is refunded in full.
  • If we cancel a project for a reason that is not your fault, we refund every amount paid for work not yet delivered.

6. Cancelling a monthly support plan

Monthly plans may be cancelled by either party with 30 days’ written notice. You are invoiced for the notice period and for nothing beyond it. Plans do not renew automatically beyond the term stated in the plan, and we never charge a cancellation fee.

7. Refunds

  • Send refund requests to contact@bonzinilabs.com, with the invoice number and the reason.
  • We answer every request within 5 business days.
  • Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 10 business days of approval.
  • Refunds are issued in the currency of the original invoice — GBP (£) or EUR (€). We do not carry the cost of exchange-rate movements between the payment and the refund.
  • Fees for work already delivered and accepted, and third-party costs we have already paid on your behalf (for example messaging or model-usage fees), are not refundable.

8. Third-party charges

Some services rely on third-party platforms billed by usage — for example the WhatsApp Business Platform or an AI model provider. Where those are billed through us, they are itemised on the invoice and passed on at cost. They are consumed as they are used and cannot be refunded once incurred.

9. If you think a charge is wrong

Contact us first at contact@bonzinilabs.com. We would rather resolve a billing question directly and quickly than through a card dispute, and we will always look at the invoice with you.
